## Overview

NYTV (New York Television) is an independent 24/7 live television station streaming culture and programming from New York City. It's a modern take on broadcast television — continuous, live, and built natively for the internet. The station positions itself as a direct answer to platform slop: free, independent, and rooted in New York now.

## Programming

A rotating 24/7 schedule spanning news, live interviews, late-night comedy and talk, archival TV, music, and a weekend animation block. Notable recurring shows include **Birds of North America** (live interviews), **Democracy Now!** (daily news), and **The Special Without Brett Davis** (late-night comedy). Civic event coverage and filler segments fill the gaps, with an on-demand library for members.
