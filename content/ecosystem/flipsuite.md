## Overview

Flippy is a crypto wallet with an AI agent, built by Flipsuite. Tell it what you want in plain words and talk to your money.

No more juggling exchange tabs and bridge sites. In a group chat or a DM, ask Flippy to "swap 50 USDC to HYPE on Hyperliquid" or "open BTC long with 15x leverage and $20 margin, and set stop loss at 110k", and your trade is one button away.

## Trade from a single message

- Swap and bridge between [dozens of chains](https://docs.flipsuite.xyz/chains), with routes compared across major exchanges, aggregators, and bridges
- Go long or short on Hyperliquid with leverage, limit entries, stacked take profits, stop losses, and partial closes
- Set spot limit orders and let Flippy fill them while you sleep
- Check charts, technical analysis, and trending tokens before you make a move
- Show off your wins with shareable PnL cards

## Move money with your people

- Send crypto, NFTs, points, or credits to a friend, a whole group, or everyone who's been active, once or on repeat
- Drop a raffle or airdrop in one message. With Mullet Club, make people hold a token or repost on X to enter

## Your wallet is waiting for you

- Every user gets a Flipwallet automatically. On almost every chain it's powered by Privy, and transfers settle onchain from your own address
- Your keys are yours to take: export them any time
- Flippy shows you the quote before money moves, and you decide how much it can send without asking

## Powered by Livepeer

Flipsuite routes its LLM inference to the Livepeer network, giving Flippy access to open-source language models running on the network's GPU supply, without Flipsuite running GPUs itself.
