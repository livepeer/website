---
title: livepeer.org/discord always points at the current invite
summary: The discord.gg/livepeer vanity URL was taken over, so the site now reads the invite live from the server's widget. Link livepeer.org/discord anywhere and it stays right without anyone editing it.
date: "2026-09-06"
---

Every Discord link on the site used to be the vanity URL, and when that was
taken over every one of them sent people to someone else's server. The site now
asks Discord's guild widget for the current invite at request time and
redirects [livepeer.org/discord](https://livepeer.org/discord) to it. Blog
posts, docs and forum threads should link that address rather than an invite.
