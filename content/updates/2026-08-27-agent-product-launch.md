The product side is done: the connector has been stable for four weeks and the
console at earlyaccess.livepeer.org has had no incident since the 12th. What is
not done is the route in for builders, and that is funded by the go-to-market
proposal, which went to the Foundation on 11 August and has not been decided.

If it is approved in the first week of September we can still land in Q3. If it
slips past that, the launch moves to October and this becomes Off track. We will
post again when there is a decision either way.
