RaidGuild was selected through the Livepeer Explorer RFP, chosen in part for a strong web3 and open-source ethos alongside the engineering work, and has since worked on the Explorer and on delegator research.

Being a collective rather than a company shapes how that work runs: a crew is assembled for an engagement rather than carried between them, and the guild is governed on-chain by its members.
