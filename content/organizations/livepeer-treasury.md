The Livepeer Treasury is the network's on-chain treasury, and the DAO that governs it is the network itself: every delegator and orchestrator, voting on treasury proposals and on governance LIPs. Proposals are submitted and tracked through the [Livepeer Explorer](https://explorer.livepeer.org/treasury).

It decides and allocates funding rather than delivering work, so it owns no roadmap commitments and never will. The bodies it funds — the SPEs among them — are the ones answerable for what lands.
