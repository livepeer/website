The Livepeer Treasury is the network's on-chain community treasury. LPT holders vote on proposals, and it is the route through which Special Purpose Entities and other ecosystem work are funded. Proposals are submitted and tracked through the [Livepeer Explorer](https://explorer.livepeer.org/treasury).

It is where funding is decided and allocated rather than a team that delivers, so the entities it funds are the ones that carry the work.
